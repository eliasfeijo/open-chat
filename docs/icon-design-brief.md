# RoomSurf App Icon Design Notes

## Core concept

A minimal icon that combines:

- discovery (map pin)
- conversation (speech-bubble implication)
- surf flow (wave)

The icon should feel modern, calm, confident, and open.

## Palette from reference

- `#0F172A` deep navy
- `#1E3A5F` navy blue
- `#2C6A8F` ocean blue
- `#74C7EC` sky cyan
- `#F6C945` sun yellow
- `#F8FAFC` off-white

## Shape and style guidelines

- Keep forms minimal and geometric.
- Avoid clutter or tiny details.
- Use flat color or very subtle depth.
- Keep the mark recognizable at small sizes (`16px`, `32px`).
- Use a map pin silhouette as the primary container.
- Integrate wave language inside the pin shape.
- Keep the composition centered and balanced.

## Variants to support

- Full color on dark background
- Full color on light background
- Monochrome dark
- Monochrome light
- Favicon-first simplification (`16px`)

## Usage targets

- App icon (iOS, Android)
- Favicon
- Social avatar
- Works on light and dark surfaces
